# Technology marks

Official technology logo shapes are sourced from the [Devicon project](https://github.com/devicons/devicon). Exact source URLs are recorded in `sources.json`; the upstream license is preserved in `LICENSE`.

The NOIR system graphic embeds these shapes in a controlled ink palette. Names and marks identify technologies; they do not imply sponsorship. Next.js retains a paper-colored letterform so its original circular mark remains legible.
