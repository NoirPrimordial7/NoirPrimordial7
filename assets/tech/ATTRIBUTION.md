# Technology marks

Official technology logo shapes are sourced from the [Devicon project](https://github.com/devicons/devicon). Exact source URLs are recorded in `sources.json`; the upstream license is preserved in `LICENSE`.

The NOIR toolkit embeds these vector shapes on individual paper tiles, preserving their source colors. Gradient and class identifiers are isolated when embedded. Names and marks identify technologies; they do not imply sponsorship. The Next.js mark retains its original dark circle and light letterform.
